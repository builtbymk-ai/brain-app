BRAIN BENCHMARK DIRECTORY
Business Revenue Assessment & Intelligence Node
Benchmark & Reference Library — Phase 1 / Phase 2 Foundation
============================================================

DOCUMENT PURPOSE
----------------
This is the reference library for BRAIN's benchmark-based analysis.

BRAIN should use these benchmarks to:

1. Compare observed business performance against relevant market benchmarks.
2. Identify Acquisition, Conversion, and Retention gaps.
3. Estimate directional opportunity where sufficient data exists.
4. Classify infrastructure and customer-experience gaps.
5. Provide evidence-backed recommendations.
6. Calculate conservative/base/upside scenarios only when the underlying inputs are sufficiently reliable.

IMPORTANT METHODOLOGY RULE
--------------------------
A benchmark is NOT proof that a business can achieve the benchmark. Benchmarks are diagnostic reference points.

BRAIN must distinguish:
[OBS] = Observed directly from the business
[BMK] = External benchmark
[ASM] = Explicit assumption
[DRV] = Derived/calculated value

Revenue estimates must NEVER be presented as guaranteed revenue.
Use language such as: "Estimated incremental revenue opportunity", "Benchmark-based opportunity", "Directional opportunity", "Pre-implementation estimate", "Potential revenue headroom"
Do NOT use: "Guaranteed revenue", "Expected guaranteed increase", "You will make $X", "This automation will generate $X"

SECTION 18 — REVENUE OPPORTUNITY MODEL INPUTS
============================================================

BENCHMARK 093 — Conversion Rate Headroom
Method: Observed conversion vs relevant benchmark range. Example: Observed 1.4%, Benchmark 2.0–3.0%, Headroom 0.6–1.6pp. Do NOT automatically use benchmark maximum.

BENCHMARK 094 — Retention Headroom
Observed repeat purchase rate vs relevant benchmark range. Example: Observed 18%, Benchmark 25%, Headroom 7pp.

BENCHMARK 095 — AOV Headroom
Observed AOV vs category benchmark. Consider: product mix, margins, bundles, shipping threshold, upsell, cross-sell, discounting.

BENCHMARK 096 — Revenue Per Visitor
Formula: Revenue / Visitors or AOV x Conversion Rate. Do not mix session-based with visitor-based denominators without documenting.

============================================================

Every benchmark should have:
benchmark_id, metric, dimension (ACQUISITION/CONVERSION/RETENTION), subcategory, value, unit, lower_bound, upper_bound, central_value, source_name, source_url, publication_date, dataset_period, sample_size, geography, industry, business_model, metric_definition, evidence_type [BMK], confidence (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW), applicability, limitations, last_verified.

============================================================
SECTION 24 — IMPORTANT BENCHMARK CONFLICTS
============================================================

Do NOT force different sources into one number. Example: Conversion rates differ across Triple Whale (2.66%), Dynamic Yield (2.72%), Shopify (3.2%), Triple Whale Shopify (1.4%) due to different datasets, years, traffic, denominators, store populations, inclusion criteria, business models.
RULE: Store benchmark with its source. Do NOT create "Official rate = X%". Select most appropriate based on business context.

============================================================
SECTION 25 — BENCHMARK SELECTION ALGORITHM
============================================================

STEP 1: Match industry (skincare/beauty before general ecommerce)
STEP 2: Match business model (DTC before generic)
STEP 3: Match traffic source (paid vs organic)
STEP 4: Match geography (US/UK/global)
STEP 5: Match date (prefer newest credible dataset)
STEP 6: Match denominator (sessions vs visitors vs users)
STEP 7: Match metric definition ("repeat customer rate" != "90-day repeat purchase rate")
STEP 8: Calculate confidence (High: industry+model+geography+current data match. Medium: some mismatch. Low: generic/old.)

============================================================
SECTION 26 — BRAIN REVENUE MODEL GUARDRAILS
============================================================

RULE 1: No revenue estimate if traffic is missing.
RULE 2: No revenue estimate if AOV completely unknown and cannot be benchmarked.
RULE 3: Do not treat benchmark maximum as target.
RULE 4: Do not treat benchmark gap as fully recoverable.
RULE 5: Apply realization factor: Conservative 25%, Base 50%, Upside 75%.
RULE 6: Apply model risk buffer: Conservative 15%, Base 10%, Upside 5%.
RULE 7: Apply automation maturity modifier: No flows x1.20, Basic flows x1.00, Mature stack x0.75.
RULE 8: Separate Revenue Creation from Revenue Protection.
RULE 9: Do not convert 90-day metric to 30-day without statistical support.
RULE 10: Do not infer quiz purchase conversion from quiz completion.
RULE 11: If observed conversion already above benchmark, shift attention to retention/AOV/acquisition efficiency/support/CX.
