============================================================
BRAIN MODEL RULES
Business Revenue Assessment & Intelligence Node
Methodological Parameters & Evidence Rules — NOT benchmarks
============================================================

DOCUMENT PURPOSE
----------------
These are BRAIN's internal model parameters and methodological
rules. They are NOT benchmarks. They are the calculation rules
that BRAIN applies AFTER evidence has been gathered and compared
against benchmarks.

These parameters define HOW BRAIN reasons, not WHAT the market
does. They can be tuned as BRAIN matures without affecting the
benchmark record library.

============================================================
SECTION M1 — EVIDENCE TAG DEFINITIONS
============================================================

[OBS]  Observed directly from the business (highest confidence)
[BMK]  External benchmark from a credible primary dataset
[ASM]  Explicit assumption (must be labelled as such)
[DRV]  Derived/calculated from observed or benchmark data
[UNVERIFIED]  Unsupported claim — MUST NOT drive revenue math

============================================================
SECTION M2 — MODEL PARAMETERS (RULES, NOT BENCHMARKS)
============================================================

P1. Realization factors
    Conservative: 25%
    Base: 50%
    Upside: 75%
    Meaning: the share of a measured benchmark gap that BRAIN
    treats as potentially addressable.

P2. Model risk buffers
    Conservative: 15%
    Base: 10%
    Upside: 5%
    Meaning: haircut applied after realization to account for
    model error.

P3. Automation maturity modifier (MULTIPLIER, not a benchmark)
    No automated lifecycle flows: x1.20
    Basic flows: x1.00
    Mature stack: x0.75
    Meaning: adjusts the confidence of opportunity sizing, NOT
    the size of the opportunity itself.

P4. Data sufficiency thresholds for revenue math
    Revenue scenario math REQUIRES:
      - traffic estimate        (any, incl. heuristic floor)
      - AOV or benchmarked AOV  (category-matched)
    If either is missing → scenarios = INSUFFICIENT_DATA.

P5. Confidence mapping
    HIGH:   OBS data + matched BMK + current dataset
    MEDIUM: some OBS data + relevant BMK
    LOW:    no OBS data, or only generic/unverified BMK

============================================================
SECTION M3 — REASONING RULES
============================================================

R1. A benchmark gap is NOT recoverable revenue.
    GAP is an input. OPPORTUNITY = GAP x realization x buffer.

R2. Do not treat benchmark maximum as target.

R3. Do not convert a 90-day metric into a 30-day metric
    without statistical justification.

R4. Do not infer quiz purchase conversion from quiz completion.

R5. If observed performance is already above the relevant
    benchmark, do NOT manufacture a conversion opportunity.
    Shift analysis to retention, AOV, acquisition efficiency,
    support, and CX.

R6. Unverified benchmarks (STATUS: REQUIRES SOURCE VALIDATION
    or [UNVERIFIED]) may be cited as context ONLY. They must
    NEVER drive quantitative revenue calculations.

R7. Missing inputs are reported as INSUFFICIENT_DATA or
    "Not found" — never silently replaced by zero or fabricated.

R8. Denominator discipline: sessions vs visitors vs users
    must not be mixed without documentation.

R9. Separation of concerns:
    Revenue Creation  = conversion, AOV, acquisition, repeat
    Revenue Protection = support, refunds, churn prevention, CX

R10. Every quantitative output must trace to at least one of:
     [OBS] input, or [BMK] benchmark with source, or a
     documented [ASM].

============================================================
SECTION M4 — SCENARIO MODEL PROCEDURE
============================================================

PROCEDURE (applied only when P4 data sufficiency passes):

STEP 1. Identify relevant benchmark (Section 25 of benchmark
        directory: industry > model > geography > date >
        denominator > definition).

STEP 2. Compute the gap between observed and benchmark.
        GAP = benchmark - observed (per metric).

STEP 3. Apply realization factor per scenario:
        Conservative GAP x 0.25
        Base         GAP x 0.50
        Upside       GAP x 0.75

STEP 4. Apply risk buffer:
        Conservative GAP x (1 - 0.15)
        Base         GAP x (1 - 0.10)
        Upside       GAP x (1 - 0.05)

STEP 5. Apply automation maturity modifier ONLY to confidence,
        not to scenario values.

STEP 6. Label every output with evidence tags and cite the
        benchmark IDs used.

STEP 7. If any required input is missing at any step, the
        scenario becomes INSUFFICIENT_DATA for that metric.

============================================================
SECTION M5 — INSUFFICIENT DATA HANDLING
============================================================

INSUFFICIENT_DATA means: BRAIN refuses to produce a number.

Required outputs when INSUFFICIENT_DATA:
  - state which inputs are missing
  - state what would be needed to produce an estimate
  - do NOT substitute a presence score, heuristic, or guess

Example output:
  "Revenue scenarios: INSUFFICIENT_DATA.
   Missing: AOV (no category benchmark match).
   Needed: product pricing page or category AOV benchmark
   to estimate incremental revenue headroom."
